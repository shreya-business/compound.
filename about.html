About

Built on a frustration.

Enterprise experience. Boutique execution. The combination traditional agencies cannot offer because their model prevents it.

The founder

Built by someone who has done the real work.

Compound. was founded by Shreya, a marketing strategist with 7+ years running campaigns at enterprise scale across McDonald's, Volkswagen, Coles, 7-Eleven, Dulux, Nivea and many more, across APAC, EMEA and North America.

After co-founding Floodgate Digital in Australia (sold to the New Wave Group), leading strategy for small, medium and enterprise clients from scratch, one thing became obvious. The most expensive thing a business can do is hire an agency that doesn't understand its commercial model.

Compound. exists to fix that. We work with a small number of clients at a time because that is the only way to do work we are proud of. We do not have a junior team running your accounts who don't understand how strategy evolves. We do not outsource strategy to templates. Every engagement gets the same senior-level thinking that drives measurable results over vanity metrics.

This is not recycled marketing theory. It is pattern recognition built across industries, teams, budgets, and real accountability.

We are based in Sydney, the Gold Coast, and Pune, and work with clients globally. If you are a B2B SaaS company, a consumer brand, or an enterprise team that needs a sharp marketing partner, we should talk.
