The questions we run before any strategy work starts

Issue No. 01 — Strategy Audit

Can you answer all six of these about your own marketing right now? Before we touch a single channel, every strategy engagement starts with the same six questions. These are the basics, the full audit goes further, but if you're missing answers to more than one or two, that's worth knowing before anything else gets touched.

  • What's your actual cost to acquire a customer, all in, not just media spend.
  • Which channel produced your last five best customers, not your last five most leads.
  • What happens to a lead in the 48 hours after they convert.
  • Who owns the number when a channel underperforms.
  • What would you cut first if budget dropped 30 percent tomorrow.
  • What are you doing purely because a competitor does it.
Most marketing problems we get called in for turn out to be clarity problems, not channel problems.

If a few of those don't have a clean answer, that's usually where we'd start.
