Our point of view

The agency model is broken. They sell activity. We sell outcomes.

Most agencies charge enterprise rates and report on follower counts and impression graphs that never connect to revenue. We built Compound. to be different: founder-led, every single time, with no junior account layer between you and the strategy.

Every channel we run and every campaign we launch has to connect to a number that matters to your business. If it doesn't, we cut it.

No vanity metrics
Followers and impressions are not in our reports unless they connect to revenue. We measure what your CFO cares about.
Senior-led, start to finish
The person who builds the strategy executes it. You always deal with a senior practitioner, not someone reading from a playbook.
Strategy first, always
Every recommendation starts with your commercial goals, not a template or a trend. We build the plan, then use the fastest tools available to execute it well.
Only what moves the needle
We will tell you to stop spending on channels that don't work, even if that means less billing. That is how client relationships last.
