01 — Origin

I started in marketing because I was obsessed with why people buy things.

Not the theory of it — the data behind it. I wanted to know what actually moves people from awareness to action, from browsing to buying, from curious to loyal. That curiosity pulled me through seven years of marketing roles that most people take twenty years to accumulate.

I've worked agency-side, brand-side, and built my own agency. I've run campaigns for global QSRs and scrappy B2B SaaS startups. I've owned paid media, SEO, CRM, content, analytics, and go-to-market strategy — sometimes all at once. What I've learned: the marketers who compound results are the ones who build systems, not just campaigns.

02 — Enterprise career

Real results at real scale.

My enterprise chapter includes leading digital strategy for McDonald's, Volkswagen, Coles, and Beiersdorf at Omnicom Media Group. This is where I learned what "accountability" actually means — when a franchise network demands you cancel a program, you don't capitulate, you fix the data.

03 — Agency chapter

I built something from scratch.

Co-founding Floodgate Digital taught me more about marketing than any client brief ever did. We went from 3 to 8 people and 30+ client accounts, with clients averaging 20% revenue growth in the first six months. Building the business meant I had to be the strategist, the account lead, the ops person, and the recruiter — often in the same week.

This is where I learned that systems beat talent every time. A well-built process, a repeatable framework, a smart workflow — those compound. Lone-wolf brilliance doesn't scale.

04 — The AI pivot

AI changes everything about marketing. And not in the way most people think.

The marketers who are going to win with AI are not the ones who use ChatGPT to write faster. They're the ones who use AI to build marketing systems that run at a scale no human team could sustain — and then apply their strategic thinking to what those systems produce.

That's what I do now. I combine 7+ years of hard-won marketing judgment with AI systems that execute at 10× the speed of a traditional agency. The result: enterprise-quality marketing at a fraction of the cost and headcount.

This is why I'm building not just a service business, but a SaaS tool, courses, and a newsletter — products that let me share this approach with more marketers than I could ever work with one-on-one.
