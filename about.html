About

Built on a frustration.

Enterprise experience. Boutique execution. The combination traditional agencies cannot offer because their model prevents it.

The founder

Built by someone who has done the real work.

Compound. was founded by Shreya Kulkarni — a marketing strategist with 7+ years running campaigns at enterprise scale across Omnicom, McDonald's, Volkswagen, Coles, 7-Eleven, and FireDaemon, across Australia and APAC.

After co-founding Floodgate Digital in Australia, leading data strategy for national retail brands at Omnicom, and building the entire marketing function for a B2B SaaS company from scratch — one thing became obvious. The most expensive thing a business can do is hire an agency that doesn't understand its commercial model.

Compound. exists to fix that. We work with a small number of clients at a time because that is the only way to do work we are proud of. We do not have a junior team running your accounts. We do not outsource strategy to templates. Every engagement gets the same senior-level thinking that drove results at Omnicom.

We are based in Sydney, the Gold Coast, and Pune, and work with clients globally. If you are a B2B SaaS company, a consumer brand, or an enterprise team that needs a sharp marketing partner — we should talk.
