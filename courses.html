Why we built this

This started with teaching SQL using AI.

When Shreya needed to learn SQL and Python to qualify for a role she really wanted, she did not enrol in a course or find a tutor. She opened an AI tool and started asking questions — explained concepts, created practice problems, checked answers, and built actual things. In four weeks, the skills were there and the role was hers.

That experience changed how we think about learning and about AI. Not as a shortcut, but as the best teacher most people have ever had. Infinitely patient, always available, able to meet you exactly where you are.

Since then, we have built marketing systems using AI that produce results no traditional agency setup could match at the same cost and speed. And we have watched other marketers, founders, and business owners struggle with AI — not because they are not smart enough, but because nobody has shown them how to use it properly for their actual work.

That is what this is. Not a theory course about AI. A practical system built by someone who runs these tools on real accounts with real budgets.

"If AI could help compress months of learning into weeks, it can help you run a better marketing function starting this week. That is what we are trying to prove with these products."
