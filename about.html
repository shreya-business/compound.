01 — Origin

I started in marketing because I was obsessed with why people buy things.

Not the theory of it. The data behind it. I wanted to know what actually moves people from awareness to action, from browsing to buying, from curious to loyal. That obsession pulled me through seven years of marketing roles that most people take twenty years to collect.

I have worked agency-side, brand-side, and built my own agency. I have run campaigns for global QSRs and scrappy B2B SaaS startups. I have owned paid media, SEO, CRM, content, analytics, and go-to-market strategy, sometimes all at once. What I have learned: the marketers who compound results are the ones who build systems, not just campaigns.

03 — Agency chapter

I built something from scratch.

Co-founding Floodgate Digital taught me more about marketing than any client brief ever had. We grew from 3 to 8 people and 30+ client accounts, with clients averaging 20% revenue growth in the first six months. Building the business meant I had to be the strategist, account lead, ops lead, and hiring manager, often in the same week.

This is where I learned that systems beat talent every time. A well-built process, a repeatable framework, a smart workflow. These compound. Individual heroics do not scale.

04 — The AI turn

AI changes what marketing can do. Most people are still using it wrong.

The marketers who win with AI are not the ones who use ChatGPT to write faster. They are the ones who use AI to build marketing systems that run at a scale no human team could sustain, and then apply their strategic thinking to what those systems produce.

That is what I do now. I combine 7+ years of hard-won marketing judgment with AI systems that execute at 10x the speed of a traditional agency. The result: enterprise-quality marketing at a fraction of the cost and headcount.

And because of that SQL experience, I know there are businesses and individual marketers who would benefit from learning this approach themselves. That is why I am building courses and a newsletter alongside the consulting work. Not to replace working with me, but to help more people get there on their own terms.
